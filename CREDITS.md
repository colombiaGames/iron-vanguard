# Credits

## Sound effects
Samples in `assets/audio/` come from the Kenney starter kits (kenney.nl), CC0 (public domain):

- Starter Kit FPS: `blaster`, `blaster_repeater`, `enemy_attack`, `enemy_destroy`, `enemy_hurt`, `jump`, `land`, `weapon_change`
- Starter Kit 3D Platformer: `break`, `coin`, `fall`, `step`
- Starter Kit Racing: `engine`, `impact`
- Starter Kit City Builder: `removal`, `rotate`, `toggle`
- Starter Kit Match 3: `match`, `tile_land`

Each in-game sound layers one or more of these samples with different pitch, filtering and timing (see `src/audio.js`).

## Music and voice
Synthesized in code with WebAudio and the browser's speech synthesis.
